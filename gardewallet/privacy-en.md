# GardeWallet privacy policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026.

GardeWallet lets people who share a home record expenses, balances, and payments. The controller is Martín Alejandro García Muñoz. You can write to [neongazeinteractive@gmail.com](mailto:neongazeinteractive@gmail.com).

## Data we store

When you create an account with email and password, or sign in with Google, we store the account id, the email address, and a visible name. Firebase Authentication handles the password. The app does not store it. If you use Google, Google gives Firebase the email and name of that account.

Inside a household we store the household name, the invite code, the members, roomie names, expenses, payments between roomies, and recurring expenses. The other members of that household can see that household data. Your account email is not shown to the other roomies.

We also store a token so we can notify you about a new expense or a payment, and the language of those notices. Google Play charges the Premium subscription. The app does not store the card. It only stores whether the household is Premium and until which date.

## Why we use it

This data is used to sign in, show the household, calculate balances, notify the other members, and know whether the household has Premium. The invite link carries the household code so another person can join after signing in.

The code and the link `gardewallet://join/{code}`, together with its QR code, are sent only when you tap share. Whoever receives them can join that household.

The CSV of the expense history is created only when the household has Premium. It is sent only when you tap export and choose who receives it. It includes the date, the concept, the amount, and who paid.

## Who processes it

Google Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Cloud Messaging store the account, the household data, and the notices. Google Play processes the Premium payment. We do not sell data. We do not use ads or analytics. We do not ask for location or contacts.

The app asks for internet, permission to show notifications, and permission for Play purchases.

## How long we keep it, and how to delete it

The data stays while the account and the household exist. You can sign out and leave the household in the app. Leaving removes your membership. Expenses already recorded stay with the household.

To delete the account, email [neongazeinteractive@gmail.com](mailto:neongazeinteractive@gmail.com) from the account email. We will delete the Firebase user and that account's document. The app does not yet have a delete button.

## Children

GardeWallet is not directed at children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, this page and the date above are updated.
